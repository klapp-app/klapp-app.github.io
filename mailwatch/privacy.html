KLapp Mail Watcher — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

This policy describes KLapp Mail Watcher, an internal operational mailbox-monitoring utility. The separate MyMood application has its own privacy policy.

Google data and purpose

The mailbox owner authorizes Google access to monitor their operational email. The tool reads messages and message metadata, including sender, subject, date, mailbox folder and message identifiers, and keeps a local operational archive. These data support incoming-mail notifications, account-support work, security notices and an audit history.

The reader uses read-only mailbox operations. It does not send email, delete messages, change labels or mark messages as read.

Storage and access

Google OAuth credentials and raw archived messages remain on the owner's designated computer. Operational metadata, private notification delivery records and monitoring state are also stored on infrastructure controlled by the owner. Access is limited to authorized operators and notification recipients.

Telegram notifications

Telegram receives the subject, sender, date and folder of messages selected for private notification. Telegram processes that notification through its service. Mail Watcher does not forward message bodies or attachments in those notifications. Notifications are not published to a public channel.

Use limits

Google mailbox data are used for this internal monitoring and support workflow. They are not sold, used to build advertising audiences, or used by this application to train general-purpose AI models.

Use and transfer of Google data must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Archived messages and operational records are retained for the owner's monitoring, support and audit needs. The mailbox owner can revoke the application's Google access through their Google Account. Revocation stops future authorized mailbox access; it does not itself remove previously archived files.

For deletion of archived records or notification copies, contact KLapp through the contact address on the KLapp homepage. Removal from the owner's archive and removal of copies delivered through Telegram are separate actions. The owner handles deletion of the archive and delivered notification copies as separate operations.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the tool's data use changes. Contact KLapp using the contact address displayed on its homepage.
