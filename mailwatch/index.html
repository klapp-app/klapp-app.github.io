KLapp Mail Watcher

An internal utility that helps KLapp monitor its own operational mailbox and receive private notifications about incoming messages.

What it does

The mailbox owner authorizes access through Google. The tool reads messages and saves an operational archive on infrastructure controlled by the owner. It does not send or delete email, change message labels, or mark messages as read.

Private Telegram notifications contain a message's subject, sender, date and mailbox folder. Message bodies and attachments are not included in those notifications.

Who can use it

This is an internal KLapp tool. It has no public registration, no shared public inbox and no public access to archived messages. Access is limited to the mailbox owner and specifically authorized operators and notification recipients.

Privacy and contact

Read the Mail Watcher privacy policy for details about Google data, storage, notifications and deletion requests. Contact KLapp through the contact address displayed on the KLapp homepage.
